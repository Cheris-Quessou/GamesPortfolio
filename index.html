Hollow Rooms

In Hollow Rooms, the player enters a creepy old house, solving intricate puzzles to progress toward the attic and reclaim a lost treasure. Designed as a technical demonstration showing how thoughtful animation and dynamic camera transitions heighten player engagement and atmospheric immersion.

Production & Key Implementations

Team Production Leadership

Fulfilling the role of Producer on a 13-member collaborative team, I served as the central node for development scheduling and communication synchronization, ensuring specialists met daily milestones.

3D Aiming Line-Plane Intersection

Engineered an aiming system mapping top-down 2D cursor coordinates into 3D gameplay trajectories. Using line-plane intersection projections, projectiles feed perfectly into crosshair positions.

OOP Extraction Objective Framework

Authored an extensible object-oriented framework to manage interactive objective milestones (planting charges, pushing carts, capturing towers) cleanly reporting callbacks to a central UI manager.

Sky Islands Showcase

Sky Islands

Sky Islands is a beautifully structured platformer where players traverse suspended landmasses, solve platform-based challenges, collect coins, and outmaneuver hunting ghost enemies. Built to demonstrate architecture-level mastery of clean Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) architectures in UE5.

Start Menu Interface
Tutorial Navigation Platforming Intro

Framework Architecture & AI Design

Clean Object-Oriented Blueprints

Hazards and map interactives leverage custom parent-child Blueprint actor inheritance. Variables such as speed, damage, and projectile patterns are exposed on abstract base classes for rapid iteration.

Moving Hazards & Trap Cannons

Blueprint Interaction Interfaces

Player interactions (activating switches, unlocking portal gateways, collecting items) are driven entirely by decoupled Blueprint Interfaces to isolate player logic and prevent circular references.

Interactive Trigger Switch & Gate Logic

UE5 Behavior Tree & Blackboard AI

Ghost enemies are driven by adaptive navigation built with Unreal Engine 5's Blackboard and Behavior Tree suites. Path nodes track dynamically, allowing enemies to shift smoothly between ambient area patrolling and chasing parameters.

Visualizing Active AI Chase & Behavior States
